Who We Are

Built on Trust, Powered by Care

Scholvia was founded by Citra Agustina with one belief: every student deserves honest guidance, not a sales pitch. We help students from Indonesia and Malaysia with university enrollment and student visa applications — step by step, from the first consultation to acceptance. Officially registered in Indonesia under PT Jembatan Global Citra (NIB: 1102250061893), operating across Jakarta and Kuala Lumpur.
