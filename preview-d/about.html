About Scholvia

The people, the purpose, and the promise behind Indonesia's most personal study abroad agency.

Citra Agustina on LinkedIn
Who We Are

Built on Trust,
Powered by Care

Scholvia was founded by Citra Agustina with a simple but powerful belief: every student deserves a guide who genuinely cares about their future. Not a sales pitch. Not a transaction. A real relationship.

We specialize in helping Indonesian students (aged 17+) navigate the journey of studying abroad. From university enrollment to visa assistance, language preparation to post-arrival support — we handle it all so you don't have to figure it out alone.

What makes us different? We do not disappear after you land. We stay with you throughout your studies, answering questions at 2 AM, helping you navigate homesickness, celebrating your wins. That is what family does.

Currently focused on Malaysia, with expanding pathways to Germany and Turkey — we are building the most comprehensive Indonesian study abroad support network in Southeast Asia.
