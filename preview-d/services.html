Everything you need to study abroad — from first dream to first day on campus. One agency, complete support.

What We Do

End-to-End Study Abroad Support

We handle every step of the journey so you can focus on your future, not the paperwork.

University Enrollment & Application

We match you with the right university based on your academic background, goals, and budget — then handle the entire application process. We submit applications on your behalf, track status, and follow up until you receive your offer letter.

University MatchingApplication SubmissionDocument PreparationOffer Letter Follow-upMalaysia · Germany · Turkey

Language Test Preparation

Most international universities require proof of English proficiency. We connect you with qualified tutors and preparation courses for major exams. For Germany-bound students, we also provide German language guidance and course referrals (A1–B2).

IELTSTOEFL iBTSAT / ACTGerman A1–B2Turkish Language Basics

Visa Consultation & Processing

With a 98% visa approval rate, our visa team guides you through every requirement. We prepare your checklist, review documents, help complete application forms, and brief you for any embassy interviews. We track your application and alert you the moment your visa is ready.

98% Approval RateStudent Visa (Malaysia)Student Visa (Germany)Residence Permit (Turkey)Interview Prep

Education-Tourism Programs

Not ready for a full degree? Our short-term programs let you experience studying abroad — language immersion, summer courses, and campus tours — before committing. Perfect for students and parents who want to see before they sign.

Short-Term CoursesCampus ToursLanguage ImmersionSummer ProgramsMalaysia Packages

24/7 Post-Arrival Support

Our support does not end when you land. This service is completely free for all Scholvia students. We coordinate airport pickup, help you find housing, guide you through university orientation, and stay on call for any challenge throughout your studies.
