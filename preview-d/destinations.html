Primary Destination

Why Study in Malaysia?

Malaysia is our most popular destination for Indonesian students, and for good reason. It's geographically close, culturally familiar, and English is widely spoken. The quality of higher education has grown significantly, with several universities ranked in the global top 500. Tuition fees are far more affordable than Western countries, and the cost of living is manageable for Indonesian families.

Why Study in Germany?

Germany is one of the world's top destinations for international students, and one of the most remarkable: most public universities charge little to no tuition — even for international students. Germany is globally recognized for engineering, science, and technology programs, and many universities now offer full English-language degree programs, removing the language barrier for many students.

Expanding Pathway

Why Study in Turkey?

Turkey offers an exceptional combination: high-quality universities, affordable fees, a rich cultural experience, and one of the world's most generous scholarship programs for international students. The Türkiye Burslari (Turkish Scholarship) covers tuition, accommodation, and a monthly stipend — making Turkey one of the most financially accessible destinations for ambitious Indonesian students.
