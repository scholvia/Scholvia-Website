Before You Reach Out

You might find your answer here. If not, we're happy to help personally.

Is consultation really free?

Yes, completely. Your first session — and any follow-up questions — are free. We only charge service fees once you formally enroll with us.

How quickly can I start?

Malaysia processing is typically 3–4 weeks. For Germany and Turkey, plan for 3–5 months. We'll give you a clear timeline in your first consultation.

What if my visa is rejected?

With a 98% approval rate, rejections are rare. But if it happens, we work with you on reapplication — at no extra charge. Your success is our success.
